# The Model · FinWise

> Module 6 · Pricing & Monetization. The pricing & packaging recommendation memo for FinWise.

## Stage + model

**Stage:** Stage 3 · Optimization, Data Point 1 (~2% Trial-to-Paid Conversion Ceiling): FinWise has successfully reached product-market fit at $10M ARR and established consistent top-of-funnel acquisition, yet trial-to-paid conversion is stubbornly capped at approximately 2%. This indicates that the core product and market demand exist, shifting our priority from initial value creation to fine-tuning monetization mechanics, user-product fit, and conversion efficiency.

Data Point 2 (60% Annual Churn / 40% Retention): With 60% of paid customers churning within their first year, FinWise is operating past the initial validation phase. The primary challenge is no longer proving the concept, but optimizing post-conversion value realization, feature engagement over time, and long-term customer retention.

**Model:** Subscription, The Core Trade-Off: While a Freemium model could potentially drive higher top-of-funnel acquisition volume, it would severely exacerbate FinWise's existing conversion and churn vulnerabilities by inviting low-intent users who never form a habit of active financial modeling. Conversely, a Transaction model does not align with how small business owners manage ongoing cash flow and financial forecasting—since forecasting is a continuous operational habit rather than a per-event utility.

Why Subscription Wins: Choosing a predictable Subscription model locks in recurring revenue and aligns directly with FinWise's software delivery model (ongoing cash-flow visibility and scenario planning). However, the critical trade-off accepted here is that we must fix our upstream acquisition intent and onboarding activation (via our M6 experiment briefs) rather than relying on pricing model gymnastics to mask product-fit friction.

_____

## Recommendation

Transition from a Rigid Time-Based Trial to a Value-Milestone-Gated Pricing & Onboarding Model.

**The pricing bet:** Is packaging matched to value?

_____

## Model signal

Targeted M4 Leading Indicator: 30-Day Active Financial Modeling Usage % (and early session core feature engagement).
How It Targets the Indicator:
Under the status quo, users face a rigid 14-day calendar countdown while navigating an unguided dashboard, causing them to churn out before experiencing tangible utility.
By gating the trial based on value milestones (such as completing the live-sync bank connection and generating the first cash-flow scenario) rather than arbitrary calendar days, we force active engagement during the critical window.
This directly drives up 30-day active modeling usage, transforming passive trial sign-ups into habitual users and lifting trial-to-paid conversion past the historical 2% ceiling.
